Business Performance Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard showcasing critical executive KPIs for business performance monitoring.

Overview
This dashboard provides a real-time, filterable view of core business metrics, built to support data-driven decision-making across revenue, customer acquisition, retention, and spending patterns.

Executive KPIs
Total Revenue — overall revenue generated
Average Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) — average cost to acquire a customer
Churn Rate — percentage of customers lost
Average Order Value (AOV) — average spend per order

Visualizations
Visuals
Revenue Trend by Subscription Type -	Revenue trend over time, split by Monthly vs Annual subscribers, with year → month drill-down
Churn Rate by Subscription Type	- Comparison of churn between subscription tiers
Revenue by Acquisition Channel	- Revenue contribution per marketing channel
Avg. Visits vs. Avg. Spend by Country -	Correlation between customer engagement and spending
Total Spend by Payment Method	- Spending distribution across payment methods
Customer Value by Country -	Geographic distribution of customer lifetime value

Interactivity
Slicers: Subscription Type, Device, Acquisition Channel, Country, Payment Method
Range filter: Purchase Year (2023–2025)
Drill-down: Year → Quarter/Month on the revenue trend chart
Cross-filtering across all visuals
